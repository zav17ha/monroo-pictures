About Monroo

We watch for
what matters.

Our story

Monroo Pictures is a photography and cinematography team focused on honest emotion, thoughtful composition and stories that still feel real years later. We work across Sri Lanka, following each celebration wherever it takes us.

Our philosophy

Less posing.
More presence.

We believe meaningful wedding imagery is created when people are free to be present. We guide when guidance is useful, step back when the moment speaks for itself, and stay alert to the gestures, expressions and details that make each celebration personal.

On your wedding day, we capture your moments as they unfold.

What shapes the work

Three things we return to.

01 · Emotion

Real over rehearsed.

We look for connection, not perfection. The small moments often become the photographs that matter most.

02 · Craft

Thoughtful, never loud.

Light, composition, movement and timing come together quietly, keeping the story at the centre of the frame.

03 · Memory

Made to outlast trends.

Our aim is a visual story that feels current today and still honest when you return to it years from now.
